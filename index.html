Data notes & methodology
  • The 17 cohort rows sum to 280 graduates. The screenshot footer says 275; the community owner confirmed the row sum of 280 on 10 October 2026.
  • Meetup attendance measures visits, not unique people. The records total 838 visits in 2025 and 263 in 2026.
  • The period selector filters education and meetups only. Merchant and funding cards remain dated snapshots.
  • Missing months are not evidence of zero activity. Cohort months and meetup event dates are separate records, not joined automatically.
  • BTC Map and Geyser figures are snapshots, not live feeds. The embedded map is provided by BTC Map.
  • CSV downloads contain only the currently visible dataset and filters. JSON includes all records and source notes.
  • Merchant names preserve the source listing, including “First Sping Enterprise”.
